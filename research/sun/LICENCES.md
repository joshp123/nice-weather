The actual downloaded files use the current **v1.1 sky-element schema** described by NOAA's [main documentation](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/global-historical-climatology-network/hourly/doc/ghcnh_DOCUMENTATION.pdf) and [sky-cover documentation](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/global-historical-climatology-network/hourly/doc/ghcnh-sky-cover-elements.pdf). ## What this licence does not establish

Cloud cover is independently observed, but **unobscured sky is not measured sunshine duration or direct normal irradiance**. The station metric must not be relabelled as the product's share of daylight hours with hour-mean DNI at least 300 W/m². The sun may be in the cloudy part of a partly cloudy sky, and thin cloud can transmit direct sun. Missing cloud records, automated sensor height limitations, the airport's distance from town and non-uniform reporting schedules are separate scientific limits.

AEMET's published sunshine totals cover **all daylight hours** over an older reference period. The derived fraction divides those totals by approximate astronomical daylight hours for a representative non-leap year, using the standard solar equation and a sunrise/sunset altitude of −0.833°. It is a broad seasonal check; it is not the product's 10:00 onward, DNI ≥300 W/m² metric. AEMET's station table does not state an irradiance threshold, so no particular threshold is assumed here.
