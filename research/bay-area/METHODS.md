# Method and sources

Written by AI on 2026.10.17 by model GPT-6 (Codex). Research only.

Evaluation is 2016–2025. Import `score()` and `local_index()` directly from `/tmp/nwf-agents/round3/opus/v3soft.py`; no scoring changes. Nice is score ≥55. Score local 10:00–21:00 inclusive, with +1-hour alignment for ERA5 irradiance and precipitation. Inputs explicitly request ERA5, GMT hourly timestamps, default Open-Meteo elevation adjustment and km/h wind. Resolved model grid coordinates/elevation are in results.json; these are not 800 m weather observations.

NOAA GHCN-Daily `.dly` inputs retain only nonmissing TMAX/TMIN with blank quality flags. Daily extrema must both exist, with TMAX > TMIN. All three reported methods use identical dates. Each month's nice fraction is multiplied by its mean 2016–2025 length (February 28.3 days), then summed: these are annualized expectations, not counts treating missing days as bad weather. The UTC input endpoint lacks the final local 2025-12-31 scoring window. Seven sites have over 98% paired days; Berkeley has 76%, especially sparse in 2024–2025. Station monthly means use these paired dates too.

For the station benchmark, compute ERA5 extrema over the entire local calendar day, respecting DST; linearly map every scoring-hour temperature between ERA5 daily Tmin/Tmax onto that day's observed Tmin/Tmax. NOAA's cooperative observing-day windows can differ from local midnight days; no hourly station curve is available to correct temperature timing. This is an ideal correction of daily extrema, not an ideal reconstruction of hourly temperature.

The reported PRISM method is a uniform monthly hourly-temperature offset: 0.5 × [(PRISM Tmax − ERA5 Tmax) + (PRISM Tmin − ERA5 Tmin)], with both climatologies from 1991–2020. PRISM is the containing 800 m cell, without interpolation. ERA5 baseline is independently fetched from daily maximum/minimum API output at the same station coordinates/default elevations; modern data remain 2016–2025, preserving ERA5's warming relative to baseline. Dew point, wind, DNI and precipitation remain unchanged in both corrections (the supplied scorer itself caps dew point at temperature).

A pre-specified sensitivity also uses separate monthly Tmax and Tmin offsets, interpolated across each ERA5 day's temperature range; see `prism_extrema` in results.json and the final monthly.csv column. These are calendar-month constants, not fitted to the evaluated 2016–2025 station data. Neither method is independently validated against withheld stations; PRISM is station-informed. In the main method, summing absolute monthly errors gives 7.7–21.4 days/year across stations, so annual errors do hide some seasonal cancellation. No observed local fog, sunshine, wind, humidity, rain or full nice-day count was obtained. No outside-US evaluation was done.

Sources: [NOAA metadata](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/ghcnd-stations.txt), [NOAA daily records](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/all/), [GHCN format/flags](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/readme.txt), [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api), [PRISM normals](https://prism.oregonstate.edu/normals/), [PRISM explorer](https://prism.oregonstate.edu/explorer/), [WorldClim](https://www.worldclim.org/data/worldclim21.html). Free public data; no keys or money used.
