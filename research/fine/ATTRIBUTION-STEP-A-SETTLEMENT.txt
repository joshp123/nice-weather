Changes: source population counts select the relative weights of independent
fine-grid climate anchors inside each quarter-degree footprint. The resulting
weather values are derived calculations, not an official GHSL product.
No endorsement by the European Union or the source authors is implied.

Unpopulated-area support: Natural Earth public-domain land geometry and the
valid-pixel support of WorldClim 1.4 and PRISM, under the terms above.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Population weights apply inside the nearest quarter-degree footprint. With
no resident population, anchors are weighted by independent land area; with
no eligible land, the own native ERA5 high/low is retained. OSM-only coastline
geometry is not an input to these temperature averages.
