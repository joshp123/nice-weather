Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information (2026).
Neither the European Commission nor ECMWF is responsible for any use that

Changes: source selection; common ERA5 monthly-mean adjustment from each
source's reference period to the actual assembled dates; temperature offsets;
felt-temperature calculations using native ERA5 dew point, wind and radiation;
resampling, quantization and tiling. Step A uses a uniform hourly air shift.
The fine high/low dataset supports independent high/low corrections with an
ordered-day exception. Build metadata records the exact sources and dates.

Changes: GDAL any-touched polygon rasterization to the regular 30 arc-second

The pure OSM membership mask and union polygon-membership mask are separately
offered under ODbL; the build manifest records their paths, provenance and hashes.
NWFT support bit 5 (value 32), the union polygon membership, is ODbL.
Weather values, source selection and source-validity properties are independent
non-OSM properties. NWFT bits 6 and 7 record WorldClim/PRISM source validity,
not OSM coastlines. Their weather-data licence remains CC BY-SA 4.0.
The ODbL membership notice also applies to that geographic property when stored
alongside weather values; file co-location does not replace component licences.
