The program embeds its land mask, time zones, clear-sky model, GRIB2/CCSDS decoder, small Natural Earth maps and Barlow fonts. It downloads ECMWF IFS data once, writes the v6 14-day records and fifteen score images, then reads the Site's `places.tsv` to write `named.bin.gz` for every named place and make tomorrow's World, Europe and USA outlooks. Named places use the shared place judgement on the forecast records and monthly offsets; score images paint the overview. Each outlook has a manifest, a 1200 × 630 preview and a 2400 × 1260 poster. Rendering happens in the program, without a browser or installed fonts. It uploads all 26 data files before its pointer, then removes runs other than the current and previous runs. Its third-party notices are at `/data/runner/LICENSE.libaec`, `/data/runner/LICENSE.pvlib` and `/data/runner/LICENSE.Barlow`.

Each daily image uses the selected land point's owning weather cell and temperature correction. The fourteen-day image uses the typical day, calculated from the shares reaching each score threshold, rather than an average. Score bytes are floored, as in the app. The named forecast file stores fourteen per-place day scores, their nice/perfect/awful shares, and the full fourteen-day judgement. It preserves the existing shared forecast model and adds no ERA5 climate correction. The runner does not write climate scores or place facts.
